## How I got this Interview (Google Big Code)
I took part in The Big Code Challenge by Google. I remember getting shortlisted for the second round and performing well in it although I didnt make it to the third round. After about 2-3 months of the challenge, a recruiter reached out on email to schedule a discussion after he took a look at my profile.
