## Why Write?

I've always kept notes — scribbled derivations in the margins of lecture handouts, half-finished markdown files scattered across my hard drive. But notes locked away in private repositories help no one, including future me who can't find them.

So this is the experiment: write clearly enough that a stranger (or six-months-from-now me) can follow along.

## What to Expect

I'll mostly write about things I'm actively working on or thinking through:

- **Experiences** — writeups on interview experiences, hackathons, competitions, etc.
- **Projects** — I work on a variety of projects, some of which I'll be writing about.
- **General notes** — a place where I can dump my thoughts and ideas, in the hope that they'll spark something in someone else.

## A Note on Frequency

I won't promise a posting schedule. I'd rather publish ten well-thought-out pieces a year than fifty half-baked ones. Quality over cadence.

See you in the next one.
